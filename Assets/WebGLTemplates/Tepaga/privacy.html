Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026. This page explains what happens to your data when you play TEPAGA! in a web browser.

Data kept in your browser

Your nickname, chosen character, settings, saved progress and records are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Online play with friends

You do not need an account. When you use "Play with friends", the game signs in anonymously to Unity's online services and uses Unity Relay to connect players. Your nickname, chosen character, position in the game, height and finish time are sent to the other players in your room. Unity processes technical data needed to provide these services; see the Unity Privacy Policy. The game engine may also process technical data under the same policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted by Vercel, which processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site. See the Vercel Privacy Notice.

Advertising

We use, or have applied to use, Google AdSense. When Google ads are shown on this site:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site or other websites.
  • Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Other banners on this site may link to external websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
